## switching a secret off and on

$ desolate domain dev secret off MYAPP-API-KEY
MYAPP-API-KEY is OFF: its value goes nowhere, and a request that carries its name toward api.example.test is refused. To switch it on: desolate domain dev secret on MYAPP-API-KEY [--ttl 30m]
[exit 0]

$ desolate domain dev secret list
MYAPP-API-KEY  ->  api.example.test  (headers: authorization)  [OFF]
MYAPP-TOKEN-TWO  ->  *.example.test
[exit 0]

$ desolate domain dev secret on MYAPP-API-KEY --ttl <duration>
MYAPP-API-KEY is on for <duration> more, then off.
[exit 0]

$ desolate domain dev secret on MYAPP-API-KEY
MYAPP-API-KEY is on.
[exit 0]
